Linky privacy policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026. Developer: Epic Project. Contact: Av.n@aol.com

Linky is a whiteboard app. A teacher draws on an Android phone or tablet, and can share the board through a link so students can watch it live in a web browser. Epic Project does not run any server for Linky and does not receive, store or sell your data.

What stays on your phone

Your boards (the drawings, pages and their titles), your pen settings and the name you type for your class are saved only in the app's storage on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes them.

What happens when you go live

  • When you tap Go live, the app asks Cloudflare for a temporary public link (a random address ending in trycloudflare.com) and connects to it. This uses Cloudflare's free "quick tunnel" service.
  • While the class is live, the current board, the name you typed, and class chat messages are sent from your phone, through Cloudflare's encrypted connection, to the students who open the link.
  • Students type a name and can send questions or messages. Those go to the teacher's phone and to the other students in the same class.
  • Nothing is kept after the class: when you tap End class, the link stops working. The app does not save chat messages or student names.
  • Anyone who has the link can watch while the class is live, so only share it with your students.

Cloudflare handles the connection as a network provider. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone.

Permissions

  • Internet: to create the class link and send the board to students.
  • Notifications and foreground service: to show "Linky is live" and keep the class running if you switch apps for a moment.

Children

The Linky app is meant for teachers. Students only open a web page and are asked for a first name, which is shown to the teacher and classmates during that class and is not stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
